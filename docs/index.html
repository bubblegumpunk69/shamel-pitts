The Red Series

Marks of RED

World premiere · Walker Art Center, Minneapolis

An Afrofuturistic meditation on the womb space — divining the effect that memory has on our experiences, senses, bodies, and imaginative possibilities. A new architecture of light, surface, and rite.

Touch of RED

Premiere · Jacob's Pillow, Doris Duke Theatre

A boxing ring becomes a stage. Two bodies — weighted with masculinity and grief — find rebirth inside its rope. Forged in the sweaty heat of physical closeness, in souls laid bare in a fraught arena illuminated by scarlet light.

The Black Trilogy

Black Hole — Trilogy and Triathlon

Concluding chapter

Three bodies. One gravity. Black Hole closes the trilogy as a collective reckoning — a triathlon of endurance, of cosmology, of arrival. "Apparitions of haunting beauty." — Fjord Review.

Black Velvet — Architectures and Archetypes

New York premiere · with Mirelle Martins

A duet of sculpted geometries between Pitts and Brazilian artist Mirelle Martins. "A haunting duet." — The New York Times.

Black Box — Little Black Book of RED

Solo · the beginning of the cycle

A solo of spoken word, evocative lighting, and video. A personal, poetic narrative on the search for identity and survival amidst impermanence.

Shamel Pitts is a Brooklyn-born artist, choreographer, and performer — founder and artistic director of TRIBE, an Afrofuturistic multidisciplinary arts collective. He trained at LaGuardia High School, Alvin Ailey, and The Juilliard School, performed seven years with Batsheva Dance Company under Ohad Naharin, and is the originator of the Black Trilogy and the ongoing Red Series.

A Brooklyn-based 501(c)(3) multidisciplinary arts collective founded in December 2019 by MacArthur Fellow Shamel Pitts. TRIBE makes multisensory works in which movement, choreography, lighting, video mapping, poetry, cinematography, costume, and music move as one body with the performers.
